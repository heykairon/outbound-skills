# Example: One of Kairon's Initiatives, Diagnosed

This is a real Kairon initiative, diagnosed with this skill on 2026-11-04. The people it went to are not named.

**The bet.** Owners of B2B service companies and founders of B2B software in Miami and Texas pay USD 250 a month for Kairon, with a weekly outbound session with a founder. The way in is what the senders already share with them: their first-degree network, and their tribes (the same universities and former employers).

## The numbers

## The replies

The interested replies did not turn into calls, and what they said explains why (translated from Spanish):

- "Let's talk in December."
- "Right now I don't need it."
- One person said they already have more leads than they can work.
- Some were unsure what Kairon sells.

## The diagnosis

**First stage that broke:** interest to meeting. The list and the opening work: a shared tribe got 1 in 2 to reply, and most of them liked the idea. What is missing is a reason to meet now. The replies say the timing is wrong, and some readers did not understand what they would be paying for.

**The fix:** an offer with a date, such as a small cohort that starts on a set day, and a first message that says plainly that Kairon is the tool their own team uses. It should move meetings from 0 of 14 to at least a few. Measure again after the next 10 interested replies.

**What not to do:** rewrite the opening or change the list. Both stages are well above the median, and a new list would only bring more interest with the same problem.

The initiative was paused on 2026-11-04 with the same conclusion, before adding more people.
