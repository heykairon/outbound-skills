# ICPs

_Last updated: 2026-10-01_

## Full-service airlines
- **Status:** validating, since 2026-10-01, because we have one airline customer
- **Companies:** full-service airlines (not low-cost), with their own in-flight safety video, worldwide
- **People:** head of brand, marketing director, head of cabin experience, in-flight product
- **Disqualifiers:** low-cost carriers, regional airlines with fewer than 20 aircraft
- **Best customers:** Aerolíneas del Plata (made their safety video in 2025, renewed for 2026)
- **Why they buy:** a rebrand, a new aircraft type, or a new route launch makes the old safety video look outdated
- **Market:** about 60 full-service airlines worldwide (IATA member list, counted 2026-10-01). Each video deal is USD 150k to 400k, and decisions take 6 to 12 months.
