# Note Patterns

Patterns, not templates. Rewrite each one in the sender's own words. A note that reads like a pattern with the name swapped in is the thing people decline.

`[brackets]` mark what changes per person. Keep it to two at most.

## Shared tribe (school, employer, city, community)

Name the shared thing. Sell nothing.

> hi [name], saw we both went through [school/company]. I'm building in [space] and trying to connect with more people from there. would be great to have you in my network, ok?

> hola [nombre], vi que los dos pasamos por [universidad/empresa]. estoy armando algo en [espacio] y quiero conectar con más gente de ahí. ¿te sumo?

## A post they wrote

Name what they said, not that they posted.

> hi [name], your point about [the specific claim in their post] matched what we keep seeing with [their kind of company]. curious whether that holds for you at [company] too?

> hola [nombre], lo que dijiste sobre [la idea concreta del post] coincide con lo que vemos en [tipo de empresa]. ¿te pasa lo mismo en [empresa]?

## A dated event (hire, raise, launch, new role)

> hi [name], saw [company] just [opened 3 SDR roles / raised the A / launched X]. that usually means [the problem it creates]. we work on exactly that, mind if I connect?

> hola [nombre], vi que [empresa] [abrió 3 búsquedas de SDR / levantó la serie A]. eso suele traer [el problema que genera]. trabajamos justo en eso, ¿te puedo sumar?

## The excuse is enough on its own

No value prop, no ask beyond connecting.

> hi [name], I'll be in [city] next week for [event]. are you going? would be good to say hi.

> hola [nombre], la semana que viene estoy en [ciudad] por [evento]. ¿vas a ir? estaría bueno saludarte.

## No note

Use it when there is no real excuse and the list cannot be improved now. Warm up first. The visit and the like or comment do the work a note would.
