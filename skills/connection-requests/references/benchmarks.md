# Benchmarks

Every number below comes from campaigns run through [Kairon](https://heykairon.com). They are our own sends, not vendor surveys. Treat them as where good campaigns landed, not as targets to hit. Compare yourself against your own baseline first.

Last updated: 2026-11.

## Funnel rates across clients

| Stage | Rate |
|-------|------|
| Invitation → accepted | ~30% |
| Accepted → replied | ~18% |
| Replied → interested | ~50% |

"Interested" means the reply was positive: they want the thing offered, or a call.

Platform ceiling: about 150 connection invitations per seat per week.

## Warm-up before the invite

| Sequence | Interested reply (of people who got a message) |
|----------|-----------------------------------------------|
| Profile visit before the invite | 19.6% |
| Cold invite, no warm-up | 12.2% |

## The note

| Note | Interested reply |
|------|-----------------|
| Ends on a question | 5.5% |
| Does not end on a question | 3.9% |

Notes of 40–55 words did better than shorter notes. Very short notes often carry no reason to accept.

## First message (after accept)

Across 5,971 first touches:

| Length | Interested reply |
|--------|-----------------|
| 40–99 words | ~10–12% |
| 100+ words | ~3–5% |

| First message | Interested reply |
|---------------|-----------------|
| Ends on a question | 9.4% |
| Does not end on a question | 8.0% |

| Campaign ask | Interested |
|--------------|-----------|
| Asks for a thing (a guide, a seat, a review) | 15.4% |
| Asks for a call | 10.0% |

## The offer

| Offer | Interested |
|-------|-----------|
| A specific session on a specific date | 17–22% |
| A report or dossier with no date | 7–9% |

## Follow-ups

| Follow-ups sent | Interested |
|-----------------|-----------|
| None | 12.5% |
| One or two | 16.2% |
| Three or more | 11.5% |
