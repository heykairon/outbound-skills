# Templates

One template for each offer, in English and Spanish, ordered by how often each offer is used across 213 of our clients' campaigns, most common first. Most people reach for the product first, so it opens the list. The offers that drew more interest come after it: prefer one of them ([benchmarks](../../initiatives/references/benchmarks.md)). Each one has the copy, the wait between steps, and a short real example. The clients are described, not named.

The rules in [message-copies](../SKILL.md) still decide. Where a template breaks one, its note says so: keep the template as it is, or fix that line, but know which one you are choosing.

## How to Read Them

- `{{…}}` is written for each lead by a model, from their profile. Keep it to the fact it names.
- `[[…]]` is filled once by the sender, for the whole campaign.
- `[one sentence on the signal that found them, or nothing]` is the reason to write, from the source of the list. With no specific reason, leave the line out.
- `---` splits the message into separate bubbles. Send one bubble if the sender writes that way.
- **Already connected?** Skip the invitation. Message 1 goes out directly.
- The waits are the defaults: up to 7 days for the accept, 1 hour, then 3 days and 7 days.

## The Product, Offered Directly

**5.7% interested (74 campaigns, 33 organizations).** When the offer has to be the product. The best of these did the same four things in the same order: a concrete fact about the person as the reason, who you are in one line, what they get in one sentence, and a free first step with "Interested?".

**Flow:** Invitation without a note → wait up to 7 days for the accept → 1 hour → message 1 → 3 days → follow-up 1 → 7 days → follow-up 2.

### English

Message 1:

```
Hi {{their first name}}, how are you?
---
I saw {{the reason: the signal or interaction that brought them, or if none, a concrete and recent fact about this person or their company that ties to what you offer, like a post, a project or an open role}}, and that's why I'm writing.
---
I'm [[your role]] at [[your company]]. [[what they get, in one sentence, addressed to them, e.g. With our tool you upload your Revit model and get the render in seconds]].
---
[[the first step, free and concrete, e.g. We have a 7-day free trial and I'll walk you through setup myself]]. Interested?
```

Follow-up 1 · 3 days:

```
{{first name}} stopping by in case my message got lost among so many. [[the first step, e.g. The free trial]] for {{their company}} is still there whenever you want it. Best
```

Follow-up 2 · 7 days:

```
{{first name}} I imagine you're swamped. I just thought it could help because of {{the same reason as message 1, in a few words}}, just let me know whenever :) Cheers, and I won't bother you again.
```

### Spanish

Mensaje 1:

```
Hola {{su nombre de pila}}, ¿cómo estás?
---
Vi que {{el motivo: la señal o la interacción que lo trajo, o si no hay, un hecho concreto y reciente de esta persona o su empresa que conecte con lo que ofrecés, como un post, un proyecto o una búsqueda abierta}}, y por eso te escribo.
---
Soy [[tu rol]] de [[tu empresa]]. [[qué gana, en una oración y en segunda persona, ej: Con nuestra herramienta subís tu modelo de Revit y tenés el render en segundos]].
---
[[el primer paso, sin costo y concreto, ej: Tenemos 7 días gratis y yo mismo te acompaño en el arranque]]. ¿Te interesa?
```

Seguimiento 1 · 3 días:

```
{{nombre}} paso por acá por si se te pasó mi mensaje entre tantos. [[el primer paso, ej: La prueba gratis]] para {{su empresa}} sigue en pie cuando quieras. Saludos
```

Seguimiento 2 · 7 días:

```
{{nombre}} imagino que debés estar a mil. Solo pensé que te podía servir por {{el mismo motivo del mensaje 1, en pocas palabras}}, cuando quieras me avisás :) Abrazo y no molesto más.
```

## An In-Person Event

**6.2% interested (26 campaigns, 10 organizations): about 9–11% when the event or a trip is the reason to write, about 4–5% to a cold ICP list or the sender's own network.** A small event in a real place, on a set date: a dinner, a tasting, a meetup. Who invites and from where, the event with a date and an easy landmark, who comes, then the ask.

**Flow:** Invitation with the note below → wait up to 7 days for the accept → 1 hour → message 1 → 3 days → follow-up 1 → 7 days → follow-up 2.

**Where it breaks a rule:** Message 1 ends with "no worries at all if the date doesn't work": in first messages, "if it's not a good time" cost 3.1 points. Follow-up 2 says it as the last message, which section 5 allows.

### English

Invite note:

```
Hi {{their first name}}, [[your name]] here, [[your role]] at [[your company]] ([[where you are, e.g. we're based in NYC]]). We're hosting [[the event, small and who it's for, e.g. a small whisky tasting for tech leaders]] on [[date]] at [[place]]. Thought you might enjoy it, happy to connect.
```

Message 1:

```
Hi {{first name}}, how are you?
---
[one sentence on the signal that found them, or nothing]
Quick one: on [[day and time, e.g. Thursday Nov 19 at 5pm]] we're hosting [[the event]] at [[place, with an easy landmark, e.g. the Campari building, right across from Bryant Park]]. [[who comes and what it's like, e.g. A small group of CTOs and engineering leaders, good whisky, relaxed evening]].
---
Would you like to come? Happy to send the details, and no worries at all if the date doesn't work.
```

Follow-up 1 · 3 days:

```
{{first name}}, did you get a chance to see this? [[how many spots are really left]] spots left for [[day, e.g. Thursday the 19th]], happy to hold one for you.
```

Follow-up 2 · 7 days:

```
Last one from me, {{first name}}: we're closing the list for [[day]]. If it's not a good time, no problem at all. Hope to meet you at one of the next ones.

## A Request for Help

**12.1% interested (10 campaigns, 8 organizations).** Learn from people who live the problem, before you sell. The five parts of [asking for help](asking-for-help.md), one sentence each. It does not name your product.

**Flow:** Invitation without a note → wait up to 7 days for the accept → 1 hour → message 1 → 3 days → follow-up 1 → 7 days → follow-up 2.

**Where it breaks a rule:** Message 1 runs well over 59 words, because it keeps the five parts whole. Its last sentence ("I know your calendar is probably packed") hands an easy out: in first messages, "if it's not a good time" cost 3.1 points. Follow-up 2 lowers the ask to a few questions in writing, which is not measured.

### English

Message 1:

```
Hi {{their first name}}, how are you?
[one sentence on the signal that found them, or nothing]
I'm trying to make [[the problem, e.g. office rental]] less of a pain for [[who, e.g. new businesses]]. We're just starting out and don't have anything to sell, but want to make sure we're building something that actually helps.

I've only ever come at it from the [[your side, e.g. tenant's]] side, and I'm having a hard time understanding how it all works from the [[their side, e.g. landlord's]] perspective. I saw you've been [[what people on their side do, e.g. renting out workspaces]] at {{their current company}} for {{how many years they have been in their current role, from their profile, in words, e.g. over five years}}, so you could really help me cut through the fog.

I know your calendar is probably packed, but if you happen to have 15 minutes to give me some feedback, I'd be truly grateful
```

Follow-up 1 · 3 days:

```
{{first name}} I'm writing again because I'm sure your inbox gets a thousand messages from people trying to sell you something. So I'm taking the liberty of bumping this up in case you see it. Best
```

Follow-up 2 · 7 days:

```
{{first name}} as expected, you must be swamped. If I can send you a couple of questions here and you answer async, that works for me too :) Cheers, and I won't bother you again.
```

### Spanish

Mensaje 1:

```
Hola {{su nombre de pila}}, ¿cómo estás?
[una oración sobre la señal que lo trajo, o nada]
Estoy tratando de que [[el problema, ej: alquilar una oficina]] sea menos complicado para [[quiénes, ej: las empresas que recién arrancan]]. Estamos arrancando y no tengo nada para venderte, pero quiero asegurarme de construir algo que realmente sirva.

Hasta ahora solo lo vi desde el lado de [[tu lado, ej: quien alquila]], y me está costando entender cómo funciona desde el lado de [[su lado, ej: quien ofrece los espacios]]. Vi que hace {{cuántos años lleva en su puesto actual, según su perfil, en palabras, ej: más de cinco años}} que [[lo que hace la gente de su lado, en segunda persona, ej: alquilás espacios de trabajo]] en {{su empresa actual}}, así que me podrías ayudar mucho a aclararlo.

Sé que probablemente tengas la agenda totalmente explotada, pero si de casualidad llegas a tener 15 minutitos para darme feedback lo agradecería de corazón
```

Seguimiento 1 · 3 días:

```
{{nombre}} te escribo de vuelta porque seguro te llegan mil mensajes al inbox de gente queriendo venderte algo. Así que me tomo el atrevimiento de reflotarlo por si lo llegas a ver. Saludos
```

Seguimiento 2 · 7 días:

```
{{nombre}} como era de imaginarse debes estar a mil, si te puedo pasar un par de preguntas por aca y me respondes async también me sirve :) Abrazo y no molesto mas.
```

## Partnership or Commission

**16.1% interested (4 campaigns, 4 organizations).** People who can resell or recommend what you sell: consultants, agencies, channel partners. Offer them a way to earn, not something to buy.

**Flow:** Invitation without a note → wait up to 7 days for the accept → 1 hour → message 1 → 3 days → follow-up 1 → 7 days → follow-up 2.

**Where it breaks a rule:** Message 1 ends on a question built to be answered "no" ("Would it be a bad idea…?"). It was barely tested. Follow-up 1 asks if they saw the message: the offer again did +4.9 points against that. Follow-up 2 asks for a no as the last message, which section 5 allows.

### English

Message 1:

```
Hi {{their first name}}, how are you?
[one sentence on the signal that found them, or nothing]
I'm reaching out because I work with [[the partner type]] in [[where]] and we're adding partners. [[what your product does, in one sentence]]. [[how the partner earns]], and [[what you do]].
---
Would it be a bad idea to walk you through the model in 15 min?
```

Follow-up 1 · 3 days:

```
Hi {{their first name}}, did you get a chance to look at my message?
```

Follow-up 2 · 7 days:

```
{{their first name}}, one last question, just in case. Is the model not for you, or is it just not the right time? Either way, great to stay in touch for possible partnerships down the road. Looking forward to your answer
```

### Spanish

Mensaje 1:

```
