# Asking for Help

Use this when the user is looking for product-market fit, for the company or for one feature. The message asks for help, and the frame is discovery. The call can end in a sale, but the message does not sell.

## The Five Parts

1. **Vision.** One sentence on the change the user is trying to make in the reader's world. "I'm trying to make desk rental less of a pain for new businesses." It says what the talk is about, without a product.
2. **Framing.** Where the user is, and what this is. "We're just starting and have nothing to sell yet." A company with products says so, and says it is moving into a new area. The framing lets them say yes without bracing for a pitch.
3. **Weakness.** The thing the user does not understand. "I've only seen it from the tenant's side." A named gap makes the request specific, and people help with a specific gap.
4. **Pedestal.** Why this reader, in particular, can close that gap. "You've rented out desks for years." It must be true of them, so the list decides who gets this message.
5. **Ask.** One clear request for a short talk, with a time frame. Ask for their advice, never for a demo.

The order can move: the framing can come after the pedestal when it reads better there. All five parts go in. Leave out "totally understand if you are busy": in our campaigns, handing the reader the easy no cost interest ([benchmarks](../../initiatives/references/benchmarks.md)).

The rules of the first message in [message-copies](../SKILL.md) still hold: the greeting, 40 to 59 words, one question at the end, no link. Each part is one short sentence:

```
Hi Ana, how are you? I'm trying to make desk rental less of a pain for new businesses. We're just starting, with nothing to sell yet. I've only seen it from the tenant's side. You've rented out desks for years and could help me see the landlord's. Do you have time for a call in the next two weeks?
```

## The Template

The same five parts as fixed sentences, with blanks. `[[…]]` is filled once by the sender. `{{…}}` is filled for each lead by a model, from their profile: only their company and how long they have been in the role. The list decides that this person sits on the other side, so the pedestal comes from the list, not from what a model guesses.

| Part | The fixed sentence |
|------|--------------------|
| Vision | "I'm trying to make [[the problem]] less of a pain for [[who]]." |
| Framing | "We're just starting out and don't have anything to sell, but want to make sure we're building something that actually helps." Only if it is true. |
| Weakness | "I've only ever come at it from the [[your side]] side, and I'm having a hard time understanding how it all works from the [[their side]] perspective." |
| Pedestal | "I saw you've been [[what people on their side do]] at {{their current company}} for {{how many years}}, so you could really help me cut through the fog." |
| Ask | "If you happen to have 15 minutes to give me some feedback, I'd be truly grateful." |

It runs over 59 words, because it keeps all five parts whole. Its last sentence opens with "I know your calendar is probably packed", the kind of easy out this page says to leave out. Cut it, or keep it and compare.

```
Hi {{their first name}}, how are you?
[one sentence on the signal that found them, or nothing]
I'm trying to make [[the problem, e.g. office rental]] less of a pain for [[who, e.g. new businesses]]. We're just starting out and don't have anything to sell, but want to make sure we're building something that actually helps.

I've only ever come at it from the [[your side, e.g. tenant's]] side, and I'm having a hard time understanding how it all works from the [[their side, e.g. landlord's]] perspective. I saw you've been [[what people on their side do, e.g. renting out workspaces]] at {{their current company}} for {{how many years they have been in their current role, from their profile, in words, e.g. over five years}}, so you could really help me cut through the fog.

I know your calendar is probably packed, but if you happen to have 15 minutes to give me some feedback, I'd be truly grateful
```

```
Hola {{su nombre de pila}}, ¿cómo estás?
[una oración sobre la señal que lo trajo, o nada]
Estoy tratando de que [[el problema, ej: alquilar una oficina]] sea menos complicado para [[quiénes, ej: las empresas que recién arrancan]]. Estamos arrancando y no tengo nada para venderte, pero quiero asegurarme de construir algo que realmente sirva.

Hasta ahora solo lo vi desde el lado de [[tu lado, ej: quien alquila]], y me está costando entender cómo funciona desde el lado de [[su lado, ej: quien ofrece los espacios]]. Vi que hace {{cuántos años lleva en su puesto actual, según su perfil, en palabras, ej: más de cinco años}} que [[lo que hace la gente de su lado, en segunda persona, ej: alquilás espacios de trabajo]] en {{su empresa actual}}, así que me podrías ayudar mucho a aclararlo.

Sé que probablemente tengas la agenda totalmente explotada, pero si de casualidad llegas a tener 15 minutitos para darme feedback lo agradecería de corazón
```

## How to Open the Call

1. **Repeat the vision, the framing and the weakness** in one or two sentences.
2. **Name who introduced you,** if someone did, and why they thought of this person.
3. **Put them on the pedestal again.**
4. **Take the reins with a specific question about their world.** For example: how one of their customers got from where it started to where it is now. Ask about what happened, never about the user's idea.

From there the user steers. Every answer says who has the problem and how they think about it. How to judge those conversations is in **[icp](../../icp/SKILL.md)**, under "No customers yet?".
