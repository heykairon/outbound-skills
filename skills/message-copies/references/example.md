# Example: One of Kairon's Campaigns, Reviewed and Rewritten

This is a real Kairon campaign, reviewed with this skill and rewritten. The people it went to are not named. The numbers come from Kairon's own data on 2026-11-09, and every campaign below is small, so read them as signals.

---

## The campaign, before

**Contratando vendedores · LATAM + ES.** It went to people at companies that had just opened a sales role, in Latin America and Spain, from 30 September to 8 October 2026. 26 people got the first message, and 1 replied with interest (3.8%).

The note:

```
Hi [name], I saw that you opened a search for a Junior Sales Development role in Colombia. I'm speaking with teams that are adding salespeople right now. How is that search going?
```

The first message, after the accept:

```
Hi [name], as I was saying: I saw a Business Development role for DACH, remote in Spain.
---
I'm building heykairon.com, a product that finds companies in your market with buying signals and writes to them with a real reason, using Claude or ChatGPT. For a team that's adding a salesperson, it keeps the top of the pipeline running from day one.
---
We're in beta and offering free trials. Would you like one to try it with your own market?
```

The follow-up:

```
[name], ¿lo de la lista no te interesa o no es el momento? Un no también me sirve.
```

## The review

| Part | Result | Why |
|------|--------|-----|
| The offer | Fail | A free trial of the product. In our data, a product pitch drew 6% interest, and free access no more than that. |
| The reason to write | Pass | The role they just opened is a fact about them, in one line. |
| The note | Partly | It carries the reason, but ends asking how their search is going: a question about their situation, before they know who is writing. |
| The question at the end | Pass | One question they can answer with a yes. |
| Length | Fail | 79 words. 40 to 59 did better. |
| Language | Fail | The first message is in English, to people in Colombia and Spain. The follow-up switches to Spanish. |
| The follow-up | Fail | It asks for a no. In our data, that brought 10 points more refusals and no more interest. |

The one change first: the offer.

## The rewrite

The offer becomes a request for help: the person is hiring salespeople, so they know the problem the product solves. This is the offer behind Kairon's best campaigns (see below). Spanish, neutral, for Latin America and Spain.

The note, only the reason:

```
Hola {{nombre}}, vi que están buscando un {{rol de ventas, de la búsqueda que abrieron}}. Me gustaría conectar.
```

The first message (41 words):

```
Hola {{nombre}}, ¿cómo estás? Vi que están buscando un {{rol de ventas}} en {{país}}. Por eso creo que me podrías ayudar: estoy armando algo nuevo para que un vendedor recién llegado tenga a quién escribirle desde el primer día. ¿Te cuento?
```

Follow-up 1, three days later, the offer again in fewer words:

```
{{nombre}}, te escribí por algo que estoy armando para equipos que suman vendedores. Me sirve tu mirada. ¿Te cuento en dos líneas?
```

Follow-up 2, a week later, a new angle built on a fact we have:

```
{{nombre}}, el contexto: heykairon.com encuentra a quién escribirle y le escribe desde la cuenta de cada vendedor. Lo usan 71 empresas, y quiero entender cómo lo usaría un equipo que está contratando. ¿Te lo muestro?
```

What changed: the free trial is out, the question about their search is out of the note, the message is in the market's language and under 60 words, and no follow-up asks for a no.

## Why this offer: what Kairon's own campaigns showed

| Campaign | Offer | People | Interested |
|----------|-------|--------|------------|
| Design partners, round 1 (two senders) | A request for help, after one fact about the person ("you prospected by hand for five years") | 40 and 47 | 22.5% and 17.0% |
| Tribes, Miami and Texas | A request for help, after a shared university in the note | 24 | 45.8% |
| People who reacted to a competitor's posts | The product, with 7 days free and the founder doing the onboarding | 35 | 25.7% |
| Contratando vendedores (the one above) | The product, with a free trial | 26 | 3.8% |

The requests for help did best. The free trial worked once: with people who had just reacted to a competitor's posts, so they were already looking for a tool. The source matters as much as the offer. The audiences differ across these campaigns, so this table is a reason to test the request for help, not proof.
