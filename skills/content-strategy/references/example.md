# Example: Kairon's Content Strategy

This is the real `.agents/content-strategy.md` of [Kairon](https://heykairon.com), still in progress. It is written for one of [Kairon's ICPs](../../icp/references/example.md), and it starts from Luciano's last 100 posts. The tactical pillar is a map of concepts: each one is an idea Luciano can teach, and each can become many posts, one angle at a time. The concepts follow the work in order: decide the strategy, find qualified leads, reach them until there is a call, and post.

---

```markdown
# Content Strategy

_Last updated: 2026-11-08_

## Subject
Go-to-market with AI, told by a founder.
- **For:** Sales teams
- **Cadence:** once a week, on Friday. Today it is about once a month.

## Pillars
### Tactical
1. GTM strategy
   - How to decide if outbound is for you
   - When to do discovery and when to sell
   - When to delegate outreach
   - What to give to AI and what not
   - How to pick a market
   - How to define your ICP
   - How to define your value proposition
   - What a lead magnet is and how to build one
   - How to set realistic projections
   - How to experiment in order and learn (initiatives)
2. Finding qualified leads
   - Why the list decides the result
   - How to order your sources, closest first
   - How to work your network and your communities
   - How to get introductions
   - How to use signals
   - How to work your competitors' audience
   - How to prospect without signals
   - How to search in Sales Navigator
   - How to qualify a list
3. Reaching leads until there is a call
   - How to do outreach in a small market
   - How to write good copy (the invitation, the message, and the initiative's strategy behind them)
   - How to write good follow-ups
   - How to make the most of your campaigns' leads (reactivation and monitoring)
   - How to maximize volume
   - How to know if it is going well or badly
4. Posting
   - Why to create content
   - How to pick what to talk about (one subject, four pillars)
   - How to set up your profile
   - How to write good posts (hooks, content, media)
   - How to measure if a post worked
   - How to turn content into leads
### Aspirational
A story (Luciano's own, a customer's, or a well-known company's) of someone who...
1. GTM strategy
   - didn't know if outbound was for them, and how they found out
   - was selling cold instead of doing discovery, and how they started getting calls
   - delegated outreach too early, and how they got it back
   - did everything by hand, and what they handed to AI
   - sold to the wrong market, and how they found the right one
   - had an ICP that was too broad, and what changed when they narrowed it
   - couldn't explain what they do, and how they found their value proposition
   - had no reason for people to say yes, and the lead magnet that gave them one
   - set impossible goals, and how they reached a projection that came true
   - tested everything at once, and how they started to learn from each test
2. Finding qualified leads
   - had good messages and bad results, until they changed the list
   - started with strangers, and what they found closer to home
   - had never written to their network, and what came out of it
   - got meetings through introductions from their own customers
   - started writing at the right moment thanks to a signal
   - found buyers in a competitor's audience
   - had no signals and still didn't sound like spam
   - couldn't find their buyer in Sales Navigator, and how they did
   - wrote to anyone, and how they started to filter
3. Reaching leads until there is a call
   - had few possible accounts and still filled the calendar
   - sent messages nobody answered, and what they changed
   - gave up after the first message, and what the follow-ups brought
   - had old leads they forgot about, and the calls that came from them
   - sent too little, and how they multiplied the volume
   - didn't know if it was going well, and how they found what was broken
4. Posting
   - didn't believe in content, and the first lead it brought them
   - posted about everything, and what happened when they picked one subject
   - had a profile that pushed people away, and what changed when they fixed it
   - wrote posts nobody read, and how they started writing posts people did
   - measured likes, and what they saw when they measured leads
   - had reach and no leads, and how they turned it into leads
### Personal
A story of Luciano's own, told as it happens, with a photo or a video. Three themes, taken from what he already posts and from what drew the most:
1. Events and trips: what happened in the room and in the hallways. Already posted: a tech week in Punta del Este (122 reactions), a weekend hackathon with 400+ sign-ups (114), San Francisco with no meetings booked (57 and 92).
2. Decisions as a founder: the hard calls, and the doubts behind them. Already posted: renegotiating contracts while friends raised millions (131), from content agency to the first enterprise customer in 18 months (123), six startups in two years and three shut down (58).
3. Habits and how to live: what he tries, and what he still doesn't know. Already posted: a wallpaper that counts the weeks of life left (76 reactions, 26 comments), "what's your purpose?" at 25 (57, 17 comments), working on Saturdays (50).
### Insight
Luciano's own opinion, with a stance. The posts that drew the most comments went against the current. Two themes:
1. The role of AI in product and sales: what changes in how teams build and sell, and what stays with people. Already posted: juniors who lean too much on AI to code (133 reactions, 62 comments), a CEO who ships features with Claude (82), how Vercel runs 20 agents with 2 people instead of 10 SDRs and AEs (60).
2. News and new tools: what just came out, and whether it matters. Already posted: the Dia browser (36 reactions, 13 comments), Google's realistic AI videos (34), LinkedIn Unwrapped is useless, so he built his own (50, 21 comments).
```
