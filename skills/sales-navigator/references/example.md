# Example: Six Real Searches, and What They Returned

These are Sales Navigator searches our clients run in [Kairon](https://heykairon.com) as lists that fill themselves every day. The clients are anonymous. Measured on 2026-11-09.

Each search is judged on three numbers:

- **Passed the ICP:** of the people the search returned, how many a qualifier judged to fit the client's ICP. The higher, the less the search wastes.
- **Accepted:** of the invitations sent to them at least 7 days before, how many were accepted.
- **Interested:** of the people who got a message, how many replied with interest.

"Leader of [function]" below stands for a block of seniority words (director, head, gerente, jefe, and their English forms) combined with AND to the function words, as in section 3 of the skill.

---

## Three searches that work

**Founders in Buenos Aires** (the fit lives in the person)
```
Current title: Founder, Fundador, Fundadora, Cofounder, Co-founder, Co Founder
Person location: Buenos Aires, Argentina
Company headcount: 1–10, 11–50, 51–200
```
2,838 returned. 38% passed the ICP. 65% of 489 invitations accepted. 16 of 289 people messaged replied with interest.

Three filters, all on the side where the fit lives. A founder title is unambiguous, so it needs no exclusions.

**Country managers in Argentina**
```
Current title: Country Manager, Gerente General, Director General, Managing Director,
  Socio, Partner, Socio Gerente, or Leader of ("administración" OR "recursos humanos" OR "human resources")
Exclude titles: asistente, assistant, analista, pasante, intern
Location: Argentina
```
790 returned. 36% passed. 68% of 38 invitations accepted.

The person who decides is named by the titles they write, and the junior titles that share those words are excluded.

**HR and safety leaders at companies with staff on many sites, Peru**
```
Current title: CHRO, or Leader of ("recursos humanos" OR "gestión humana" OR "capital humano"
  OR "talento humano" OR "SST" OR "SSO" OR "SSOMA" OR "seguridad y salud" OR "capacitación")
Exclude titles: practicante, asistente, analista, intern, student, consultor, consultora
Location: Peru
Exclude industries: Banking, Financial Services, Capital Markets, Higher Education,
  IT Services and IT Consulting, Staffing and Recruiting, Non-profit
```
1,240 returned. 23% passed. 54% of 235 invitations accepted. 15 of 113 messaged replied with interest (13%).

The ICP is companies with operational staff on many sites. No filter says "many sites", so the search excludes the industries that have HR leaders but no field staff: banks, universities, IT services, staffing firms.

## Three searches that waste

**E-commerce leaders in Argentina: one broad word**
```
Current title: CMO, or Leader of ("ecommerce" OR "e-commerce" OR "comercio electrónico"
  OR "marketing digital" OR "negocio digital" OR "digital")
Location: Argentina
```
980 returned. 12 passed the ICP (1.2%).

"digital" on its own matches any leader with digital in the title, such as digital transformation, digital banking or digital products. That one word is the most likely reason almost nobody passed. Cut the broad word, or exclude what it brings in.

**Manufacturers in Wisconsin: seniority with no function**
```
Seniority: Owner/Partner, CXO, Vice President, Director
Company location: Wisconsin
Company industry: Food and Beverage Manufacturing, Dairy, Packaging and Containers, ...
```
2,939 returned. 14% passed. 10% of 40 invitations accepted.

Seniority alone finds every leader in those companies, in finance, plant, quality and HR alike. The search never says which function buys, which most likely explains the low numbers. Add the function words.

**Car dealership owners in Argentina: an industry that brings the makers**
```
Current title: Gerente General, Dueño, Owner, Socio Gerente, ... or Leader of ("comercial" OR "ventas" OR "sales")
Exclude titles: plan de ahorro, posventa, marketing, ...
Location: Argentina
Industry: Retail Motor Vehicles, Motor Vehicle Manufacturing, Wholesale Motor Vehicles and Parts
Exclude companies: Stellantis, Toyota Argentina, Volkswagen Group Argentina, ...
```
4,774 returned. 9% passed.

The ICP is dealerships, which sit in Retail Motor Vehicles. Manufacturing and Wholesale bring the car makers and parts distributors too, and the search tries to exclude the makers one by one. Keep only the industry where the buyer is.
