# Hook Patterns

Patterns, not templates. Every `[bracket]` must be filled with something real from the user. If you don't have it, ask.

The hook is the first two lines, inside about 200 characters.

## Tactical

> [Who: you, or a real person] [did what, concretely: "cold-messaged 507 supply chain people with nothing to sell"]. [The result, in a number: "They got ~35 calls"]:
> [The concept or method they applied, in one line.]

> [Quién: vos, o una persona real] [hizo qué, concreto: "le escribieron en frío a 507 personas de supply chain sin nada para vender"]. [El resultado, en un número: "Sacaron ~35 calls"]:
> [El concepto o el método que aplicaron, en una línea.]

Not "Here are the 4 steps". The steps come inside the story.

## Aspirational

> [Client] went from [A] to [B] in [time].
> [What changed for them, said plainly.]

> [Cliente] pasó de [A] a [B] en [tiempo].
> [Qué cambió para ellos, dicho simple.]

## Personal

> [Concrete time: "Two years ago today"], [what happened, said plainly].
> [One line that holds back the rest.]

> [Tiempo concreto: "Hace dos años"], [lo que pasó, dicho simple].
> [Una línea que guarda el resto.]

## Insight

> [The news or the change, with its source].
> [Your proof: why you see it up close], and here's what it changes:

> [La noticia o el cambio, con su fuente].
> [Tu prueba: por qué lo ves de cerca], y esto es lo que cambia:

## Weak hooks to rewrite

| Weak | Why | Fix |
|------|-----|-----|
| "I've been thinking a lot about sales lately." | No anchor | Open with the event that made you think it |
| "Most people get outbound wrong." | Abstract, could be anyone | Name the number or the case that proves it |
| "It's not about the message. It's about the timing." | "X, not Y" reveal | State what happened and what you learned |
| "Want to know the secret to more replies?" | Self-answered question | Give the result and the method directly |
