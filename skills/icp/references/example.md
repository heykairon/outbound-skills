# Example: Kairon's ICPs

This is the real `.agents/icp.md` of [Kairon](https://heykairon.com). It builds on [Luciano's identity](../../outbound-context/references/example-identity.md). The three ICPs come from Kairon's own client tiers and from who really bought and used Kairon in each paying account. Customers are described by what they do, not by name.

---

```markdown
# ICPs

_Last updated: 2026-11-07_

## Sales teams
- **Status:** validated, since 2026-11-07, because 9 customers like these pay and get results.
- **Companies:** sell to a buyer you can find on LinkedIn by title and company: a role inside an organization (B2B), or a professional buying for themselves, like a career program for senior executives. At least one person prospects full time (an SDR or BDR), or a sales team of two or more. Traction: paying customers and a result they can show again and again, with some authority in their field. A market big enough to keep writing to for months: thousands of buyers, not a few hundred.
- **People:**
  - Buys: the founder or CEO, or whoever leads sales or business development. Real titles of the buyers: Founder & CEO · CEO · Co-Founder · Senior Sales & New Business Manager · BDR Director · Founding Head of Sales · Head of Commercial.
  - Uses it: the sales team. Real titles: Account Executive · Sales & Business Development Representative · Sr BDR · Country Manager · Jefe de ventas · Representante comercial · Chief Revenue Officer.
- **Disqualifiers:** buyers you can't find on LinkedIn (shops, restaurants, mass consumer); nobody in sales (the founder alone is the third ICP); no traction yet; a market of a few hundred accounts.
- **Best customers:** a fintech infrastructure company (4 seats: account executives and a BDR); a global payroll platform (a BDR director and BDRs; its event invitations got 6–11% interested replies); a career-coaching firm for senior executives (9.5% interested per contact); a marketing consultancy (CEO, CRO and a sales rep; 7.5%); a company with country managers in Chile, Venezuela and Peru (4.5%); 4 more.
- **Why they buy:** they have concrete growth objectives, and they need a system that reaches them in a predictable way. They need a steady flow of leads, and outreach by hand doesn't scale across several seats.
- **Business approach:** start with a small account, show the value, then grow into more seats and more volume. Teams added seats 2 to 8 weeks after they started.
- **Sources** (closest first):
  1. They already know you: tried Kairon and didn't pay; conversations that stopped; ex-customers.
  2. Network and tribes: founders and sales leaders in Luciano's 6,100 connections; ITBA, FIUBA, ORT, Innovid, TRIBU Tech Latam; Latin America and Spain (same language, same culture); Argentinians in the US.
  3. They come to you: people who react to Luciano's posts; his 6,400 followers.
  4. Warm intros: customers who refer peers (the best customers first); Kairon's advisors.
  5. Signals: hiring SDRs or BDRs, opening a country or naming a country manager, a funding round; engaging with competitors (Origami, Regrow, Gojiberry); active on LinkedIn, going to events, traveling.
  6. Lookalike: a search for the buyers' titles above, at companies like the best customers.

## Recruiting firms
- **Status:** validating, since 2026-11-07, because only one firm so far (it brought three client accounts).
- **Companies:** recruiting firms, or independent recruiters, that fill roles for several client companies at once. The people they hire can be found on LinkedIn by title. Their clients hire all the time (high turnover, or fast growth).
- **People:**
  - Buys: the founder of the recruiting firm.
  - Uses it: the firm's recruiters, and later the client's own talent team. Real titles: Founder · Recruiter & Talent Sourcer · Talent Discovery Manager · Talent Discovery Lead · Talent Discovery Consultant · Talent Acquisition Specialist · Head Hunter.
- **Disqualifiers:** firms or recruiters that fill few roles a year: not enough volume to automate.
- **Best customers:** a recruiting firm whose founder recruits for several clients (14% interested per contact). She brought three of her clients into Kairon, each with its own account. One of them, a digital agency's in-house talent team, got 26% and went from 4 to 17 seats in 4 weeks.
- **Why they buy:** to automate the whole candidate sourcing process: finding the people, writing to them, and following up, without a recruiter doing it by hand.
- **Business approach:** the firm is the door into its clients. Start with the firm's own account, show results on one client, and the firm brings that client in with its own account and seats. One firm has brought three so far.
- **Sources** (closest first):
  1. They already know you: recruiters and recruiting firms that tried Kairon and didn't pay; conversations that stopped.
  2. Network and tribes: founders of recruiting firms and recruiters in Luciano's 6,100 connections.
  3. They come to you: recruiters who react to Luciano's posts about sourcing.
  4. Warm intros: the firm's founder refers other firms and recruiters she knows; her clients' talent teams refer peers.
  5. Signals: firms with many open roles at once; firms hiring recruiters; engaging with posts from sourcing tools [open: which ones].
  6. Lookalike: founders of recruiting and staffing firms, and heads of talent acquisition, at companies like the best customers.

## Founders who sell alone
- **Status:** validated, since 2026-11-07, because 19 customers like these pay.
- **Companies:** small agencies or very early startups that sell to a buyer you can find on LinkedIn by title and company, and whose offer already brings traction with little volume. Nobody else does sales: the founder runs the tool alone, whether or not they did outreach before.
- **People:**
  - Buys and uses it: the founder. Real titles: Founder · Fundador · Cofundador · CEO & Co-founder · Founder & CEO · Managing Director · Gerente Comercial · Growth Partner.
- **Disqualifiers:** buyers you can't find on LinkedIn; no clear offer yet.
- **Best customers:** an AI consultancy (6.8% interested per contact, with a dated stand at a tech week); four more founders working alone at 4–5%.
- **Why they buy:** to automate prospecting in a very easy way, instead of paying for several complex and more expensive tools.
- **Business approach:** self-serve. They set it up alone, on one seat at US$49 a month. What keeps them is how easy it is and the price. Later the founder may add a salesperson, slowly: one seat at a time.
- **Sources** (closest first):
  1. They already know you: founders who tried Kairon and didn't pay; conversations that stopped.
  2. Network and tribes: founders in Luciano's 6,100 connections; ITBA, FIUBA, ORT, TRIBU Tech Latam (a founders' community); Argentinians in the US. Kairon's design partners round to its own network reached 87 people, and 3 companies signed up and pay.
  3. They come to you: founders who react to Luciano's posts.
  4. Warm intros: founders who use Kairon refer other founders; Kairon's advisors.
  5. Signals: founders who post about prospecting or outreach; engaging with competitors' posts (Origami, Regrow, Gojiberry, Trigify); a new agency or a launch; going to startup events.
  6. Lookalike: founders and CEOs of agencies and startups with 1 to 50 people, at companies like the best customers.
```
