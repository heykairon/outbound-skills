# Example: Luciano's LinkedIn Profile

This is the real review and rewrite of Luciano's profile, built on [Kairon's value propositions](../../value-prop/references/example.md). The customers whose logos are on the banner are not named here.

---

## The review, before

| Part | Result | Why |
|------|--------|-----|
| Photo | Partly | Real and looking at the camera, but from February 2024, with a hand on the face. |
| Banner | Not seen | The profile data does not include it. A screenshot was needed. |
| Headline | Fail | "Co-Founder at Kairon" says his title, not what a stranger gets. |
| About | Fail | It opened on the technology ("agentic infrastructure"), spoke to two tribes at once (sales and marketing teams), told no turning point, and some of its numbers came from his previous company. |
| Together | Fail | The profile was in English, and his messages go out in Spanish. Luciano chose English, because his recent posts are in English too. |

The one change first: the headline, because it shows next to every post, comment and invitation.

## The banner

| Part | Text |
|------|------|
| The tribe | FOR B2B SALES TEAMS |
| The value proposition | Prospect on LinkedIn and email without leaving Claude. Find qualified leads ready to buy. Send them a message worth reading. |
| Social proof | "Trusted by" and eight logos: seven customers, and one company whose founder allowed it although it does not use Kairon. That is why the label says "Trusted by" and not "Teams using Kairon". |
| Call to action | heykairon.com, with Kairon's logo |

Built in Kairon's own brand (dark background, its serif and its one orange), at 3168 × 792, twice LinkedIn's 1584 × 396. The bottom-left corner is left empty, because the profile photo covers it.

## The headline

The first proposal was the proof: "172 meetings for B2B sales teams, straight from LinkedIn with AI". Luciano wanted the product in it, and wrote his own:

> Building an MCP that prospects for you without leaving Claude | Co-founder of Kairon

It says what he builds and where it lives, the same promise as the banner. It opens on what he does, not on what the reader gets. That was his call.

## The about

The first draft read like a pitch. Luciano asked for a story, cut his earlier career to one line, wrote the turning point himself, closed with the line from his launch video, and added a call to action.

```
I'm an engineer who ended up in sales.

I spent almost 4 years creating software for sales teams.

One day I decided to quit my job and start a new startup. I realized startup sales teams were using at least 4 different tools, and still weren't getting good results. So I decided to change that.

That's how Kairon started. It lives inside Claude: you tell it who you sell to, and it finds the right people, reads what they posted, and writes to each one like a person would.

In 3 months we got 60+ clients getting weekly sales calls, consistently.

Want to try it with your own market? It's free for 7 days: heykairon.com
```

What was left out: numbers from his previous company, and a closing about what he believes. The turning point is his own words; the model had left it open rather than write one.
