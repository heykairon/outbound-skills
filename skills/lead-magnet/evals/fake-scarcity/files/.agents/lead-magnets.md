# Lead Magnets

_Last updated: 2026-10-20_

## Las 10 facturas más viejas
- **For:** Wholesale distributors, Argentina
- **Problem it solves:** "No sé a quién llamar primero"
- **Next problem it shows:** chasing them by phone every week, which the paid offer removes
- **Type and form:** reveal a problem + service
- **What they get:** a one-page list of their 10 oldest unpaid invoices, ordered by who to call first, from an export of their accounts receivable. Five minutes to read.
- **How we make it per person:** they send the accounts receivable export from their ERP. We order it by age and amount. 20 minutes each.
- **How we say it:** "tus 10 facturas más viejas y a quién llamar primero"; "a quién cobrarle primero esta semana"; "tu lista de cobranza de la semana"
- **Next step:** a 20-minute call to show how the reminders would go out on their own.
