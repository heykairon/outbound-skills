# Value Proposition

_Last updated: 2026-10-02_

## For Wholesale distributors, Argentina
- **The line:** We help wholesale distributors in Argentina get paid sooner without chasing late payers by phone, like Distribuidora Andina, which went from 52 to 34 days to collect in 4 months.
- **Before:** "los lunes mi cuñado se pasa la mañana llamando clientes para que paguen". Nobody knows which invoices are late until the month closes.
- **After:** they collect sooner, nobody has to chase, and the owner stops financing their own clients.
- **Proof:** Distribuidora Andina, 52 to 34 days to collect in 4 months. Mayorista Del Sur, from 3 people on collections to 1.
- **Time to first result:** three old invoices collected in the first month, at Andina.
- **Effort we remove:** reminders go out on their own by WhatsApp and email.
- **Why us:** payments reconcile with their ERP every night.
- **Weakest driver:** proof. Only two customers, both in Buenos Aires.
