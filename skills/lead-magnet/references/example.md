# Example: Kairon's Lead Magnets

This is the real `.agents/lead-magnets.md` of [Kairon](https://heykairon.com). Each one starts from a problem in [Kairon's value propositions](../../value-prop/references/example.md). The numbers come from what Kairon really sent from its own accounts.

---

```markdown
# Lead Magnets

_Last updated: 2026-11-07_

## Your next leads, with a reason to write
- **For:** Sales teams
- **Status:** testing since 2026-07-03. 521 people were offered it, 7.9 interested per 100. Made for the person and announced in the invitation: 11 per 100, and 2 of 172 became paying customers. A generic report of rates or signals: 5 per 100, and no customers. Offered for later ("I'll have the list ready before your new hire starts"): 0.6 per 100. The first tests went mostly to founders who sell alone, with about 10 leads. The current iteration gives much more value: 100+ leads, to buyers with a large market (the Sales teams ICP).
- **Problem it solves:** finding the right people to write to. ("I go profile by profile and build my list.")
- **Next problem it shows:** writing to all of them, every day, and following up without losing anyone. Kairon does that.
- **Type and form:** a sample + information (a PDF report)
- **What they get:** 100+ people who fit their ICP and just showed a signal, each with the signal, its date, and a first message ready to send, in a PDF.
- **How we make it per person:** read their site and posts for who they sell to; run Kairon's signal search on that ICP; keep the people who pass it; write one first line each from the signal. About 10 minutes each.
- **How we say it:**
  - "I made a report for [company] with 100+ qualified leads from signals, each with a message ready to send. Want it?"
  - "Teams that showed a signal, the person who owns it, a first line each. Send it?"
  - [open: a third]
- **Next step:** after they read it, offer the 7-day trial to send to those same leads. [open: a true reason to do it now]

## Pipeline with AI and LinkedIn: a session with peers
- **For:** Sales teams
- **Status:** testing since 2026-11-04. 131 people invited, too soon to judge.
- **Problem it solves:** they have no consistent system to experiment and learn. ("We're really just guessing.")
- **Next problem it shows:** running that system every week, on every seller's account. Kairon does that.
- **Type and form:** reveal a problem + information (a live session)
- **What they get:** 40 minutes with sales leaders from Latin America and Spain, on how they build pipeline with AI and LinkedIn.
- **How we make it per person:** the session is the same for all; the invitation names what the person's team does.
- **How we say it:**
  - "On Thursday 12/11 we run a 40-minute session with sales leaders from LatAm and Spain on how they build pipeline with AI and LinkedIn. Save you a seat?"
  - [open: two more]
- **Next step:** [open]
```
