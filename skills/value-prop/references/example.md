# Example: Kairon's Value Propositions

This is the real `.agents/value-prop.md` of [Kairon](https://heykairon.com), one value proposition for each of [Kairon's ICPs](../../icp/references/example.md). The quotes come from calls with customers, and the numbers come from what those customers really sent. Customers are described by what they do, not by name.

# Value Proposition

_Last updated: 2026-11-07_

## For Sales teams
- **The line:** We help sales teams hit their growth targets with a steady flow of qualified leads, applying strategies validated with companies like theirs. The first interested reply comes in the first 2 weeks, without manual prospecting or spam.
- **Before:**
  - They have no consistent system to experiment and learn: they try something, don't know what worked, and start over. ("We never thought it through strategically, so we never ran campaigns smoothly." · "We tried a campaign with lead magnets, another with a diagnosis, another with just conversation." · "We're really just guessing.")
  - Prospecting eats their sellers' time: finding the right people and the right signals to write to them, building lists, tracking follow-ups, instead of selling. ("It takes a lot of time to find the right person, and then to send, send, send messages." · "We have follow-up dates in Monday, but it still needs a human." · "I don't have the bandwidth.")
  - The tools are hard to use, too expensive, or they need several of them for one simple job. ("I'm just trying to use too many outbound tools." · "We're paying quite a lot more for a tool that is more limited, and I'm not getting much out of it.")
  It costs them their sellers' hours, campaigns that teach them nothing, and paying for several tools. (Spanish quotes translated.)
- **After:** a number of meetings they can count and plan on ("500 messages bring me one meeting, done"), so they can open new countries and new industries. The leader knows what volume brings a meeting, and the team hits its targets.
- **Proof:** teams like theirs booked 172 meetings from 19,767 invitations, about one meeting every 115 invitations. 36% accept the invitation, 35% of those reply, and 35% of the replies are interested: 4.5 interested replies per 100 invitations, more than twice our median client (2.0).
- **Time to first result:** the first interested reply in about 2 weeks (17 days for the median team).
- **Effort we remove:** finding people one by one, writing and following up by hand, and juggling several tools. Each seller sends from their own account, in their own voice, and nothing goes out without their approval.
- **Why us:** Kairon runs from the team's own Claude or ChatGPT. It reads LinkedIn for the right moment and the true reason to write (who changed jobs, who reacts to a competitor, who is hiring), checks each person against the ICP, and writes from each seller's account.

## For Recruiting firms
- **The line:** We help recruiting firms fill more roles for more clients by automating candidate sourcing. The first interested candidates in about a week, without searching and writing to them one by one.
- **Before:**
  - They source candidates by hand: search profile by profile, write to each one, and follow up one by one until they send their CV.
  It costs them their recruiters' hours, roles they don't fill on time, and clients they can't take on. (From Luciano, not yet in a client's own words.)
- **After:** every role gets a steady flow of interested candidates, so the firm takes on more clients with the same team, and brings its clients into the same system.
- **Proof:** a recruiting firm and its client's talent team sent 1,809 invitations. 52% accepted, and 20.5 of every 100 people contacted were interested in the role, ten times our median client (2.0). They booked 27 interviews.
- **Time to first result:** the first interested candidate in 7 to 11 days.
- **Effort we remove:** searching for candidates profile by profile, writing to each one, and following up to get the CV.
- **Why us:** Kairon runs from the recruiter's own Claude or ChatGPT. It searches LinkedIn for the people who fit each role, writes to them from each recruiter's account, and follows up until they answer.

Assumed: not yet heard from a buyer.

| "We already pay for LinkedIn Recruiter." | Kairon searches with the firm's own Recruiter seat. |

## For Founders who sell alone
- **The line:** We help founders who sell alone get a steady flow of meetings without spending their day on prospecting, with one easy tool instead of several complex ones. The first interested reply in about 11 days, without lists or messages by hand.
- **Before:**
  - Their customers come only from referrals, so growth depends on others. ("Until now, all my clients came by referral.")
  - Outreach is a side job that eats their day: searching one by one, writing, and following up by hand. ("I'm literally going to LinkedIn by hand, looking for founders one by one." · "I'm spending almost an hour a day on this cycle." · "I'm doing follow-up 100% by hand, and when it grows, it's impossible to keep up.")
  - They juggle several tools that don't talk to each other, or that break. ("I used Apollo, Clay, HubSpot, and Dripify." · "I started with one software, then moved to another... In the end, everything gets lost.")
  - The automation they tried sounds generic, so it doesn't convert. ("I tried to automate it, and it still sounds very generic.")
  It costs them an hour of their day, the sales they never get to, and paying for several tools. (Spanish quotes translated.)
- **After:** outreach runs without them, and they know how many messages bring a sale. They spend their time on the product, and hire a seller later, when the numbers say so.
- **Proof:** founders like them sent 11,446 invitations. 34% accepted, 30% of those replied, and 27% of the replies were interested: 2.9 interested replies per 100 invitations, about one and a half times our median client (2.0). They booked 69 meetings.
- **Time to first result:** the first interested reply in about 11 days for the median founder.
- **Effort we remove:** building lists by hand, writing each message, following up, and connecting several tools.
- **Why us:** Kairon runs inside the founder's own Claude or ChatGPT. They ask "connect me with this ICP", and it finds the people, writes in their voice from their own account, and follows up. One tool, US$49 a month.
