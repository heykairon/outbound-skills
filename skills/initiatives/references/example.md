# Example: Kairon's Initiatives

This is the real `.agents/initiatives.md` of [Kairon](https://heykairon.com). All six initiatives go after the same one of [Kairon's ICPs](../../icp/references/example.md), and each changes only the source, closest first. The first offers one of [Kairon's lead magnets](../../lead-magnet/references/example.md); its source was sized on a real sample, 46 of Luciano's 1st-degree connections qualified against the ICP. The second is manual: introductions from customers, built from their work history. The third retries a bet that failed, and says what is different this time. The fourth tests a signal Kairon collected before but never wrote to. The fifth is small on purpose: inbound adds up even when it is little. The sixth is the backfill: a lookalike with the lowest priority, so the others take the seat first.

---

```markdown
# Initiatives

## Sales teams, Luciano's network
- **Status:** active
- **The bet:** Sales teams + a report of 100+ qualified leads, each with a personalized message ready to send + Luciano's 1st-degree connections who never heard from Kairon. Made for the person, the leads report drew 11 interested per 100 and became Kairon's only lead magnet that brought paying customers. Sales teams need many more leads than a founder, so the report gives 100+ instead of 10.
- **What changes:** who gets it and how big it is. The report was tested with 10 leads, mostly on founders who sell alone, through invitations to strangers. Now: sales teams, 100+ leads, people who already know Luciano. Three things change at once, so the result will say whether the bet works, not which change made it work.
- **Until:** 2026-12-18
- **What we expect:** about 570 people. A sample of 46 says 63% pass the ICP and 45% of those were never contacted: 2,016 × 63% × 45%. At 11 interested per 100, about 63 interested, so 63 reports to make. At 2 customers per 172 contacted, about 6 new paying teams. One seat writes about 150 a week, so four weeks. All rates come from founders and invitations, so they are an assumption until the first review. One report takes about 10 minutes, so 63 reports are about 10 hours of work.
- **Campaigns:** one campaign, from Luciano's seat. The first message offers the report. The report is made only after a yes.

## Sales teams, intros from customers
- **Status:** active
- **The bet:** Sales teams + an introduction, asked by name + people in the networks of Kairon's best customers who worked with them. A happy customer who vouches for Kairon opens a door no message can, and it is the warmest source left after Luciano's own network.
- **What changes:** the source, and that it is manual. No campaign: Luciano writes to each customer and asks for the people on the list by name.
- **Until:** 2026-12-18
- **What we expect:** 23 asks through 7 customers. Each name passed the ICP filters and shares a company and the same years with the customer (checked on both profiles), because being connected is not the same as knowing each other. Kairon has no measured rate for introductions, so the number of meetings is [open] until the first review.
- **Campaigns:** none. A list with three columns per person: who introduces them, the shared work history, and how strong the tie is.

## Sales teams hiring prospectors
- **Status:** active
- **The bet:** Sales teams + a report of 100+ qualified leads, each with a personalized message ready to send + companies that posted an opening for an SDR, BDR, lead generation or outbound role in the last 30 days, in Spain, Mexico, Colombia, Argentina and Chile. A team that hires a prospector needs leads for that person's first day.
- **What changes:** this was tried before and failed ("Hiring sellers", ended 2026-10-24: 163 contacted, 1 interested). Three things are different. The offer: then a list "ready before your new hire starts", an offer for later that drew 0.6 interested per 100; now the report, made today, which drew 11. The roles: then any seller, account executives included; now only prospecting roles. The filter: then a test ICP; now Sales teams, which drops staffing agencies (about a quarter of the postings) and companies over 5,000 people. The job ad picks who to write to this week. It is not the hook, because last time it made Kairon read as a recruiter.
- **Until:** 2026-12-18
- **What we expect:** about 100 to 150 new companies a month, so about 150 to 225 by the date, from a sample of Argentina's postings (about 1 in 4 qualifies). One or two buyers each, about 300 people. At 11 interested per 100, about 33 interested and 33 reports to make. At 2 customers per 172 contacted, about 3 new paying teams. The rates come from founders reached by invitation, so they are an assumption until the first review.
- **Campaigns:** one campaign from Luciano's seat, fed every week by a sweep of new postings. The first message offers the report.

## Sales leaders in a new role
- **Status:** active
- **The bet:** Sales teams + a congratulation on the new role, with no offer + people who moved into a sales leadership role (head, director, VP or manager of sales, growth, business development or revenue, CRO, country manager) in the last 90 days, 2nd degree, in Argentina, Mexico, Spain, Chile and Colombia. A leader in a new role is building a plan and is open to new tools. That is how Luciano met the buyer of one of Kairon's Tier A customers: he congratulated him on his new job.
- **What changes:** the source and the offer. Kairon ran a job-change signal before (June and July, another ICP) but never wrote to the people it found, so this is the first real test. Messages with no offer drew 8.8 interested per 100 (398 contacted).
- **Until:** 2026-12-18
- **What we expect:** about 2,930 people match the search, renewing every month; in a sample of 40 about half fit, so about 500 a month. The limit is the seat, not the audience: about 450 invitations by the date. At 8.8 per 100, about 40 interested. If half book a meeting (an assumption), about 20 meetings. The 1st-degree connections who changed roles (122) stay in the network initiative, where the new role decides who gets written to first.
- **Campaigns:** one campaign from Luciano's seat, fed every week by the same search. The invitation or first message congratulates them on the new role and asks one question about it.

## Sales teams who engage with Luciano
- **Status:** active
- **The bet:** Sales teams + a message about what they engaged with, with no offer + people who reacted to or commented on Luciano's posts, or viewed his profile, and have never talked with him. They showed interest first, so writing to them is the most natural opening there is.
- **What changes:** the source. Kairon already watches who engages with competitors and other voices, but until now not with Luciano's own profile.
- **Until:** 2026-12-18
- **What we expect:** small but steady. Luciano's latest post drew about 95 people and about 1 in 6 looked like the ICP; his profile drew about 25 visits in a recent window, about 5 of them ICP. So about 15 to 20 people per post, and at 4 posts a month, about 60 to 80 people a month. Messages with no offer drew 8.8 interested per 100, so a few interested a month.
- **Campaigns:** one campaign from Luciano's seat, fed every day by two signals: engaged with Luciano's profile, and viewed Luciano's profile. The campaign skips anyone he already talked to.

## Sales teams in Argentina and Colombia, researched one by one
- **Status:** active, low priority
- **The bet:** Sales teams + a message researched for each person, as custom as it can be, that offers the report of 100+ leads + a lookalike search of Argentina and Colombia: founders, CEOs and sales leaders at companies of 11 to 500 people, without the industries that never fit (about 63,000 people). It covers the rest of the market, and lets the research do the work a signal would do.
- **What changes:** the source and the message. The lookalike is the farthest source, so it comes last and runs as backfill: when the other initiatives run out of people on a given day, this one uses the seat's spare capacity. The message is researched per person before it goes out.
- **Until:** 2026-12-18
- **What we expect:** in a sample of 40, 21 passed the ICP (53%), about 14 of them a clear fit, so the audience never runs out. Its volume is whatever the seat has left after the other initiatives [open: measured at the first review]. At 11 interested per 100 and 2 customers per 172 contacted, both measured with founders by invitation, so an assumption until the first review.
- **Campaigns:** one campaign from Luciano's seat, with the lowest priority. A lookalike source walks the search every day and fills the list; the ICP drops the ones that do not fit and anyone outside Argentina and Colombia. A research step reads each person and company (what they sell and to whom, the sales team and its growth, whether the founder posts, traction) and writes the first message from it, offering a report of 100+ leads built for their buyers.
```
