# Example: Kairon's Initiatives

This is the real `.agents/initiatives.md` of [Kairon](https://heykairon.com). All three initiatives go after the same one of [Kairon's ICPs](../../icp/references/example.md), and each changes only the source, closest first. The first offers one of [Kairon's lead magnets](../../lead-magnet/references/example.md); its source was sized on a real sample, 46 of Luciano's 1st-degree connections qualified against the ICP. The second is manual: introductions from customers, built from their work history. The third retries a bet that failed, and says what is different this time.

---

```markdown
# Initiatives

## Sales teams, Luciano's network
- **Status:** active
- **The bet:** Sales teams + a report of 100+ qualified leads, each with a personalized message ready to send + Luciano's 1st-degree connections who never heard from Kairon. Made for the person, the leads report drew 11 interested per 100 and became Kairon's only lead magnet that brought paying customers. Sales teams need many more leads than a founder, so the report gives 100+ instead of 10.
- **What changes:** who gets it and how big it is. The report was tested with 10 leads, mostly on founders who sell alone, through invitations to strangers. Now: sales teams, 100+ leads, people who already know Luciano. Three things change at once, so the result will say whether the bet works, not which change made it work.
- **Until:** 2026-12-18
- **What we expect:** about 570 people. A sample of 46 says 63% pass the ICP and 45% of those were never contacted: 2,016 × 63% × 45%. At 11 interested per 100, about 63 interested, so 63 reports to make. At 2 customers per 172 contacted, about 6 new paying teams. One seat writes about 150 a week, so four weeks. All rates come from founders and invitations, so they are an assumption until the first review. [open: how long one report takes]
- **Campaigns:** one campaign, from Luciano's seat. The first message offers the report. The report is made only after a yes.

## Sales teams, intros from customers
- **Status:** active
- **The bet:** Sales teams + an introduction, asked by name + people in the networks of Kairon's best customers who worked with them. A happy customer who vouches for Kairon opens a door no message can, and it is the warmest source left after Luciano's own network.
- **What changes:** the source, and that it is manual. No campaign: Luciano writes to each customer and asks for the people on the list by name.
- **Until:** 2026-12-18
- **What we expect:** 23 asks through 7 customers. Each name passed the ICP filters and shares a company and the same years with the customer (checked on both profiles), because being connected is not the same as knowing each other. Kairon has no measured rate for introductions, so the number of meetings is [open] until the first review.
- **Campaigns:** none. A list with three columns per person: who introduces them, the shared work history, and how strong the tie is.

## Sales teams hiring prospectors
- **Status:** active
- **The bet:** Sales teams + a report of 100+ qualified leads, each with a personalized message ready to send + companies that posted an opening for an SDR, BDR, lead generation or outbound role in the last 30 days, in Spain, Mexico, Colombia, Argentina and Chile. A team that hires a prospector needs leads for that person's first day.
- **What changes:** this was tried before and failed ("Hiring sellers", ended 2026-10-24: 163 contacted, 1 interested). Three things are different. The offer: then a list "ready before your new hire starts", an offer for later that drew 0.6 interested per 100; now the report, made today, which drew 11. The roles: then any seller, account executives included; now only prospecting roles. The filter: then a test ICP; now Sales teams, which drops staffing agencies (about a quarter of the postings) and companies over 5,000 people. The job ad picks who to write to this week. It is not the hook, because last time it made Kairon read as a recruiter.
- **Until:** 2026-12-18
- **What we expect:** about 100 to 150 new companies a month, so about 150 to 225 by the date, from a sample of Argentina's postings (about 1 in 4 qualifies). One or two buyers each, about 300 people. At 11 interested per 100, about 33 interested and 33 reports to make. At 2 customers per 172 contacted, about 3 new paying teams. The rates come from founders reached by invitation, so they are an assumption until the first review.
- **Campaigns:** one campaign from Luciano's seat, fed every week by a sweep of new postings. The first message offers the report.
```
