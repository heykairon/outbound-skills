# Outbound Context

_Last updated: 2026-09-10_

## Value proposition
- **Who we help:** small B2B companies in Argentina that collect by bank transfer and chase late payers by phone.
- **The problem, in their words:** "persigo clientes por WhatsApp para que me paguen".
- **Before → after:** Distribuidora Andina went from 52 to 34 days to collect in 4 months.
- **Why us, not the next vendor:** reminders go out on their own by WhatsApp and email, and payments reconcile with their system.
